# Exercise 01 — Phishing Email Analysis

## Objective

Analyze the email provided in the C3SA exercise and identify potential security risks.

## Scenario

The email claims to be from the IT Department and asks employees to provide their username and password.

## Is the Email Suspicious?

Yes.

## Red Flags

1. The email requests sensitive credentials.
2. Users should not provide passwords through email.
3. The request should be independently verified.
4. The sender and communication should be carefully checked.

## My Response

I would not provide my username or password.

I would verify the request through an official IT communication channel and report the suspicious email through the appropriate security process.

## Security Concepts

- Phishing
- Social Engineering
- Credential Theft
- Security Awareness

## Lessons Learned

- Never share passwords through suspicious emails.
- Verify unexpected requests for credentials.
- Be careful with requests that ask for sensitive information.
- Report suspicious communications.
